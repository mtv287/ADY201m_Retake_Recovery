# TV2 Insights: EDA & RQ1, RQ2

### RQ1 Findings (Mean Score Delta)
*   **Observed Difference**: The overall mean score delta between the first failed attempt and the next observed attempt is +1.79 points (calculated from 10,786 complete score pairs).
*   **Course Variation**: There is a notable observed difference across courses. Specific quantitative courses show distinct patterns in score improvement compared to theoretical subjects.

### RQ2 Findings (Recovery Rate Factors)
*   **Initial Failure Type**: There is an observed relationship between the reason for initial failure (e.g., PE, FE, Attendance) and the subsequent recovery rate.
*   **Initial Score**: Higher initial failing scores are associated with a higher probability of passing the next observed attempt.

### Limitations & Warnings
*   **Missing Score Limitation**: Analysis involving score delta strictly utilizes only the 10,786 complete score pairs.
*   **Small-Group Warning**: Subgroups with n < 100 have been filtered.
*   **Regression to the Mean**: Score improvements may be partially associated with regression to the mean.
*   **Association Not Causation**: All findings denote observed relationships. No academic factor evaluated here *causes* recovery.
